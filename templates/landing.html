The course uses Six Sigma as the operating system for developing practical judgment.

Four levels

Start where your knowledge actually is.

White Belt introduces the language. Yellow Belt builds participation skills. Green Belt develops project leadership. Black Belt adds advanced analysis and complex organizational change.

Designed for operations

The organization is part of the process.

Every case includes process performance, stakeholder incentives, competing priorities, and the politics that determine whether an improvement survives implementation.
